01 — About the faculty

FIT is one of the faculties of the University of Moratuwa, and the first faculty of its kind in Sri Lanka's national university system.

It was established in June 2001 to meet the country's growing need for IT professionals. Today it runs three internal honours degrees, an external degree, and postgraduate programmes from MSc to PhD.

“Built in 2001 for a country learning to code.”

01 — Origin

Since 2001

Founded in June 2001 as the first IT faculty in a Sri Lankan state university, built to train the people the country's tech industry was waiting for.

02 — Departments

Three Departments

  • Information Technology
  • Computational Mathematics
  • Interdisciplinary Studies

Code, maths and the human side of technology, taught under one roof.

03 — Degrees

Three Honours Degrees

BSc Hons in Information Technology, in IT & Management, and in Artificial Intelligence, plus the external Bachelor of Information Technology.

04 — Research

Beyond the Degree

MSc in IT, MSc in AI, MPhil and PhD programmes since 2004, with the Information Technology Research Unit (ITRU) behind them.
